Quiet Achievers

Quiet Achievers is still my favourite project to date. It combines three of my passions: photography, story-telling and human connection. I moved to Melbourne, Australia for 2 months in 2023 to work on this.

*

Quiet Achievers is a platform that shares and celebrates the authentic stories of a diverse group of Asian-Australians. From politicians to startup founders to artists to climate activists - we explore the complexity in each individual’s relationship with their cultural identity, and look into how that has shaped them as a person, and how they live today.

Tech for Social Good

TSG taught me a lot - how gruelling but satisfying it is to build a movement from what was just an idea, how I’m personally interested in pursuing ‘tech’ from an innovation perspective, all the ups and downs of building with close friends.

*

TSG is Australia's first nonprofit for young Australians interested in responsible tech. We work with leaders in tech, government and the private sector to deliver initiatives such as fellowships and community events, and are home to a diverse and interdisciplinary community of young Australians.

Photography

I picked up film photography in 2018. It's my first proper passion that I've kept up and continuously iterated on. I specialise in street photography and enjoy capturing how humans interact with our physical spaces. Usually built, sometimes natural. I'm getting into portraiture a lot more.

Simply put, I enjoy finding the beauty in the mundane and everyday.

In 2024, my photography was exhibited at Carriageworks, one of Sydney's largest multi-arts precincts at Blackbird’s Sunrise festival.
